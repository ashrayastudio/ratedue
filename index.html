How to calculate the annual gap

For a per-visit or monthly price: (Your standard price − the client’s price) × expected yearly occurrences. For the monthly clean above: ($150 − $120) × 12 = $360.

The estimate assumes the same service and frequency across a year. It does not predict bookings, client acceptance or extra income. RateDue compares your prices; you decide whether a change makes sense.

From review to conversation

How do you tell an existing client about a price increase?

Keep the service, new price and effective date clear. Give the client time to respond, and check any notice terms in your agreement before sending.

RateDue Pro includes editable, offline message templates. Nothing is sent automatically.

An illustrative message to adapt

Hi [client name], thank you for trusting me with your monthly cleaning. From [effective date], the price for our usual service will be $150 per visit, up from $120. Please let me know if you’d like to discuss the change before then. I appreciate working with you.

Replace the placeholders and adjust the details to match your service and agreement before sending.

Before your next review

Client-rate review questions

Should I raise every client’s price?

No. A difference is a prompt to review, not an instruction to increase. You can mark a rate reviewed without changing it, or intentionally grandfather it. Consider whether the service scope is comparable before deciding.

How often should I review my rates?

Choose a review rhythm that fits your service and agreements. A review is a chance to look again at scope, your current standard and the client’s rate; it does not have to result in a change. RateDue supports review dates and local reminders.

Does RateDue tell me what to charge?

No. You enter your own standard price and each client’s agreed price. RateDue helps organize the comparison; it does not supply market prices or calculate the right price for your business.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

The free version supports one active service and three active client rates. Full JSON backup and restore are free. Pro is a one-time lifetime unlock for unlimited services and client rates, portfolio summaries, staged plans, message templates, advanced reminders, rate history and CSV reports. The purchase screen shows your local price.

Is this invoicing or bookkeeping software?

No. RateDue focuses on reviewing client rates and planning changes. Keep using your existing tools for invoices, payments and bookkeeping.

Where is my client information stored?

On your iPhone, unless you choose to export it. RateDue has no app account or backend. You can export a JSON backup and restore it later. Read the privacy policy or visit support for more detail.

Private by architecture
